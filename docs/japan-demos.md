The route inspiration is *[Lonely Planet Japan](https://shop.lonelyplanet.com/products/japan)*, 19th edition (July 2026), EPUB ISBN 9781806535927 (print ISBN 9781838698140):

| Book itinerary | Pages | Suggested allocation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The Classic Route | 28–29 | Tokyo 3 days, Kyoto 3, Nara 1, Himeji 1, Hiroshima 1, Miyajima 1 |
| Kyūshū & Yakushima | 36–37 | Fukuoka 2 days, Nagasaki 2, Kurokawa Onsen 1, Kumamoto 1, Kagoshima 2, Yakushima 2 |

Each book route allows ten days. The JSON files preserve those destination/day allocations. The classic demo uses Kyoto as the Nara day-trip base, continues from Himeji to Hiroshima, and returns from Miyajima to Hiroshima on the last day. It does not include the book's optional return to Tokyo. The southern demo finishes on Yakushima. Read the individual [classic provenance](../trips/japan-classic/PROVENANCE.md) and [Kyushu provenance](../trips/japan-kyushu/PROVENANCE.md) for the overnight choices and coordinate sources.

The calendar uses the same invented May 10–19, 2027 dates to make switching alternatives easy. Each file has ten listed days and nine overnight gaps. The classic demo has three overnight bases; the southern demo has six. Those base choices are implementations of the suggested routes, not hotel recommendations.

## Comparable illustrative budgets

Every amount is for one person in EUR. Both plans use €45 per living day and €60 per accommodation night, giving `10 × €45 + 9 × €60 = €990`. Route-specific transport placeholders add €240 for the classic demo and €275 for the southern demo, yielding €1,230 and €1,265. No reservation or payment status is implied.

Transport estimates sum to 645 minutes (10 h 45 min) and 1,050 minutes (17 h 30 min), respectively. Overview rounds estimated headlines to hours; open Details for the precise sums. These examples deliberately make transfer time and frequency visible. They are not quoted fares, timetable claims, route recommendations or evidence that one plan is currently cheaper.

Daily living includes broad meal, local transit and attraction allowances. The separate travel blocks cover the illustrated intercity connections; do not count those again in daily living. International arrival, travel home, and any onward leg beyond the final destination are outside both plans. Replace dates, prices and transport assumptions with researched values for your own trip.
