# Kyushu & Yakushima: demo provenance

This is an original Trip Atlas adaptation of **Kyūshū & Yakushima**, in *Lonely Planet Japan*, 19th edition (July 2026), Selena Takigawa Hoy et al., published by Lonely Planet Global Limited. The [publisher's book page](https://shop.lonelyplanet.com/products/japan) identifies the edition and print ISBN 9781838698140. The consulted EPUB gives ISBN 9781806535927.

The source is the **Itineraries** chapter, pp. 36–37. Its EPUB chapter is `06-itineraries-jap19.xhtml`, route anchor `ref-81`, with page anchors `page_36` and `page_37`.

The guide explicitly allows ten days and allocates them as follows:

| Source destination | Days | Demo days |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Fukuoka | 2 | 1–2 |
| Nagasaki | 2 | 3–4 |
| Kurokawa Onsen | 1 | 5 |
| Kumamoto | 1 | 6 |
| Kagoshima | 2 | 7–8 |
| Yakushima | 2 | 9–10 |

The destination order and day allocations follow the guide. This demo chooses rail and bus travel on the mainland, a jetfoil crossing to Yakushima, and Miyanoura as the island base. Its overnight placement produces **10 listed days and 9 nights**: Fukuoka 2, Nagasaki 2, Kurokawa Onsen 1, Kumamoto 1, Kagoshima 2, Yakushima 1. It omits optional detours and ends on Yakushima. The daily titles and prose are original.

The shared start date, **10 May 2027**, only demonstrates the calendar. Transport durations are rounded planning inputs, including an allowance for local access and connections; they are neither published timetables nor verified future services. Walking and sightseeing time is excluded from the transport total. The long Nagasaki–Kurokawa transfer intentionally travels through Hakata, without treating the connection as another overnight destination.

The transport choices were checked against primary sources: [JR Kyushu's transfer guide](https://www.jrkyushu.co.jp/english/guide/train_seat.html) for the Takeo-Onsen interchange; the [Kurokawa Onsen Ryokan Association](https://www.kurokawaonsen.or.jp/en/access/) for bus links from Hakata and Kumamoto; and the [jetfoil operator](https://www.tykousoku.jp/fare_time/) for the Kagoshima–Miyanoura connection. These establish the route pattern, not availability on the illustrative dates. Verify services when making a real plan.

All money is **illustrative EUR per person**, authored for comparing the demos rather than taken from the guide or current fare quotes. Both demos use €45 per day for food, ordinary local transport and routine sightseeing, plus a €60 accommodation share per night. The separately listed route journeys are excluded from that daily living input. Kyushu & Yakushima adds €275 for those journeys: **€450 living + €540 accommodation + €275 transport = €1,265**. Arrival before day 1, the return crossing or other onward travel after day 10, insurance and shopping are outside this comparison. No rail pass, reservation or payment is implied.

Map pins are approximate orientation points, rounded from embedded maps on primary tourism and operator pages: [Fukuoka](https://www.japan.travel/en/destinations/kyushu/fukuoka/fukuoka-city/), [Nagasaki](https://www.japan.travel/en/destinations/kyushu/nagasaki/nagasaki-city-and-around/), [Kurokawa Onsen](https://www.japan.travel/en/spot/643/), [Kumamoto](https://www.japan.travel/en/spot/ma_245/), [Kagoshima](https://www.japan.travel/en/destinations/kyushu/kagoshima/kagoshima-and-around/), and [Miyanoura Port](https://www.tykousoku.jp/dock/miyanoura.html). They do not identify lodging.
