# The Classic Route: demo provenance

This is an original Trip Atlas adaptation of **The Classic Route**, in *Lonely Planet Japan*, 19th edition (July 2026), Selena Takigawa Hoy et al., published by Lonely Planet Global Limited. The [publisher's book page](https://shop.lonelyplanet.com/products/japan) identifies the edition and print ISBN 9781838698140. The consulted EPUB gives ISBN 9781806535927.

The source is the **Itineraries** chapter, pp. 28–29. Its EPUB chapter is `06-itineraries-jap19.xhtml`, route anchor `ref-52`, with page anchors `page_28` and `page_29`.

The guide explicitly allows ten days and allocates them as follows:

| Source destination | Days | Demo days |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Tokyo | 3 | 1–3 |
| Kyoto | 3 | 4–6 |
| Nara | 1 | 7 |
| Himeji | 1 | 8 |
| Hiroshima | 1 | 9 |
| Miyajima | 1 | 10 |

The destination order and day allocations follow the guide. This demo chooses Kyoto as the overnight base for the Nara day trip, continues from Himeji to Hiroshima on day 8, and returns from Miyajima to Hiroshima on day 10. It omits optional detours and the guide's optional final return to Tokyo. These transfer and overnight choices produce **10 listed days and 9 nights**: Tokyo 3, Kyoto 4, Hiroshima 2. The daily titles and prose are original.

The shared start date, **10 May 2027**, only demonstrates the calendar. Transport durations are rounded planning inputs, including an allowance for local access and connections; they are neither published timetables nor verified future services. Walking and sightseeing time is excluded from the transport total.

All money is **illustrative EUR per person**, authored for comparing the demos rather than taken from the guide or current fare quotes. Both demos use €45 per day for food, ordinary local transport and routine sightseeing, plus a €60 accommodation share per night. The separately listed route journeys are excluded from that daily living input. The Classic Route adds €240 for those journeys: **€450 living + €540 accommodation + €240 transport = €1,230**. Arrival before day 1, onward travel after day 10, insurance and shopping are outside this comparison. No rail pass, reservation or payment is implied.

Map pins are approximate orientation points. Tokyo, Kyoto and Hiroshima reuse the existing Japan demo coordinates. Nara uses the [Kofukuji area](https://www.japan.travel/en/spot/1012/), Himeji uses the [castle area](https://www.japan.travel/en/spot/1030/), and Miyajima uses the [Itsukushima Shrine area](https://www.japan.travel/en/spot/887/), rounded from the maps embedded by Japan National Tourism Organization. The latter also confirms the rail-terminal/ferry access pattern; these pins do not identify lodging or exact station entrances.

No book text, artwork, maps or other assets are bundled. This independent example is not affiliated with or endorsed by Lonely Planet.
